We welcome interested developers to participate in the project and make contributions. 

1. Follow the instructions at [Installing Apache Texera using Docker](/docs/getting-started/installing-using-docker/) to install Texera on your laptop using Docker. Get familiar with the system as a user.
1. Follow the steps in the [Contribution Guidelines](/docs/contribution-guidelines/) to get on board and raise a pull request PR).  It will be reviewed by the team before it can be merged.
1. Check issues in https://github.com/apache/texera/issues and see if you can fix some of them.  Focus on the easy ones first.

After making enough contributions, you may be promoted to be a committer.  If you prefer, we can also add you to our Slack workspace and invite you to join our meetings.
